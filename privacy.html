Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect your name, email, and Telegram bot tokens that you provide. All data is stored securely on Supabase infrastructure.

2. How We Use It

To run your storefront and provide the service. We never sell your data to third parties.

3. Security

Passwords are hashed by Supabase Auth. Bot tokens are protected by Row Level Security so only you can access them.

4. Cookies

We use only essential cookies for session management. No tracking or advertising cookies.

5. Your Rights

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time by contacting support.

6. Contact
